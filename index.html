How we think

Fewer things, made well.

Four rules. We break everything else.

Start with the toy.

Before it's a game, it's a toy. If it isn't fun to poke at with grey boxes, a coat of paint won't save it.

Feel is a feature.

The snap of a tap, the weight of a fall, the timing of a bounce. We tune it until your hands notice before your eyes do.

Fun in the first minute.

If the first minute isn't fun, the rest never gets a chance. We teach by letting you play, not by making you read.

Ship it, then listen.

Real players beat our opinions. We put it out, watch people play, and keep making it better.
